About GY Summit 2026

The GY Summit is the annual gathering of young people from all churches within PCEA Githunguri Presbytery.

The summit brings together youth for spiritual growth, worship, leadership, Bible teaching, sports, missions, networking and fellowship.

Our desire is to raise a generation that lives in holiness, walks in the fear of God and transforms society through Christ-centered leadership.
