About GY Summit 2026 – Githunguri Presbytery Youth Summit

The Y-Summit, also known as GY Summit, Githunguri Y Summit and the PCEA Githunguri Presbytery Youth Summit, is the annual gathering of young people from all churches within PCEA Githunguri Presbytery.

The summit brings together youth for spiritual growth, worship, leadership, Bible teaching, sports, missions, networking and fellowship.

Our desire is to raise a generation that lives in holiness, walks in the fear of God and transforms society through Christ-centered leadership.

GY Summit 2026 (Y-Summit Season 4) – Frequently Asked Questions

What is GY Summit (Y-Summit)?

GY Summit, also called Y-Summit or the Githunguri Presbytery Youth Summit, is the annual gathering of young people from churches within PCEA Githunguri Presbytery. It brings participants together for spiritual growth, worship, Bible teaching, Christian leadership, sports, missions and fellowship.

When is GY Summit 2026 (Y-Summit Season 4)?

Y-Summit Season 4 2026 runs from 15 to 19 December 2026. Check this website and the announcements for the confirmed programme and any updates.

Where will the Githunguri Y-Summit take place?

The venue for Y-Summit Season 4 2026 is Kambui Girls' Senior School in the Githunguri area of Kiambu County, Kenya.

Who is eligible to register?

The summit is for young people from churches within PCEA Githunguri Presbytery. Participants can use the online registration page to create an account.

What activities are planned?

The summit programme brings together worship, Bible teaching, spiritual growth, Christian leadership, missions, fellowship and sports competitions.

How do I register for GY Summit 2026?

Use the official participant registration page, complete the personal, church and account details, and submit the form to create your participant account. Payment is confirmed at the admission desk on arrival.
