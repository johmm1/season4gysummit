Githunguri Youth Summit 2026 Registration

Register online for GY Summit 2026, also known as Githunguri Y-Summit or Y-Summit Season 4. The PCEA Githunguri Presbytery Youth Summit takes place from 15–19 December 2026 at Kambui Girls’ Senior School in Kiambu County, Kenya.

Young people from churches within PCEA Githunguri Presbytery can use this official registration form to create a participant account. Enter your personal and church details, then create a secure password. If you have already registered, log in to your participant account.
